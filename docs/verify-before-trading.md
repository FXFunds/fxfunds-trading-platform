# Verify Before Trading with FXFunds

A neutral checklist for anyone researching FXFunds, including people searching "Is FXFunds legit?" or "FXFunds reviews".

## Checklist

1. **Legal entity** — Find the registered company name, registration number and jurisdiction in the website footer or terms.
2. **Regulation** — Look up that entity on the regulator's own public register (not a link supplied by the broker). Useful registers:
    - US: [NFA BASIC](https://www.nfa.futures.org/BasicNet/) (CFTC/NFA)
    - UK: [FCA Register](https://register.fca.org.uk/) and [FCA Warning List](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/warning-list-unauthorised-firms)
    - Global: [IOSCO I-SCAN investor alerts](https://www.iosco.org/i-scan/)
    - India: [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/) and the [RBI Alert List](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx) of unauthorised forex platforms
3. **Risk disclosure & client agreement** — Read both in full, especially leverage, fees and withdrawal terms.
4. **Independent reviews** — Read reviews on independent sites, not testimonials published by the platform.
5. **Test withdrawal** — Withdraw a small amount before depositing more.
6. **Contact details** — Confirm a working support email and physical address.

## Warning Signs (Any Broker)

- Guaranteed or unusually high returns
- Pressure to deposit quickly or add funds to "unlock" a withdrawal
- Account managers contacting you through messaging apps
- No verifiable licence on a regulator's register
