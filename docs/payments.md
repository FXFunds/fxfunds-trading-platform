## Before You Deposit

- Confirm the exact coin **and** network on the platform before sending — sending on the wrong network can mean permanent loss.
- Read the withdrawal conditions in the client agreement (minimums, fees, verification/KYC requirements, processing times).
- Make a small test withdrawal before committing larger amounts.
- Keep records of every transaction hash and support ticket.
